# Part 1 (unknown automotive · 2018)

## Insight
TODO: Verify brand. Multi-part automotive film series where product performance and engineering craft are demonstrated through visually demanding cinematography.

## Mechanic
A multi-part film series (Part 1 / Part 2) showcasing automotive performance through craft filmmaking — cinematography, sound design, and vehicle movement are primary communication vehicles.

## Why it worked
Product as proof (P14): performance claims are demonstrated visually rather than stated. Craft as message (P08): the production quality signals the product quality. The two-part format creates anticipation and extends earned attention across multiple releases.

## Steal
Split a performance story across two films to build anticipation. The craft of the film should mirror the craft of the product — a badly shot car film undermines its own claim of precision engineering.

# Part 2 (unknown automotive · 2018)

## Insight
TODO: Verify brand. Continuation of the multi-part automotive film series (see Part 1). The two-part structure extends audience engagement and creates a narrative arc.

## Mechanic
Second installment of the performance film series, continuing the visual and sonic language established in Part 1.

## Why it worked
Product as proof (P14): sustained performance demonstration across two films provides more evidence than a single spot could. Craft as message (P08): the investment in a multi-part series signals engineering confidence. The continuation format rewards audience attention.

## Steal
Two-part film campaigns extend reach by creating a reason to return. The second part should introduce a complication, a new perspective, or a resolution — not simply repeat the first. Narrative continuity turns a campaign into a story.

# Dead Mouse Theater (unknown · 2016)

## Insight
TODO: Verify brand. The absurdist execution features a dead mouse as the central character — a darkly comic premise that flips the standard aspiration of CPG advertising.

## Mechanic
A series of short comedic films featuring a dead mouse protagonist placed in mundane domestic scenarios, played straight for maximum absurdist effect.

## Why it worked
Absurd as carrier (P11): grotesque or taboo imagery treated with deadpan sincerity disables audience defenses. Long-form drama (P16): the extended comedic premise builds via slow escalation. The contrast between mundane setting and bizarre protagonist creates a comedic gap.

## Steal
Mundane product category + genuinely bizarre protagonist = shelf-break comedic interruption. The more seriously you play the absurd premise, the funnier and more memorable it becomes.
