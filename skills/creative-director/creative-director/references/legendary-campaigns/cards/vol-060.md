# The Trash Isles (LadBible · 2017)

## Insight
The Great Pacific Garbage Patch is larger than France, but because it belongs to no nation, no international body is responsible for it — and no legal mechanism exists to force action. The absence of sovereignty was the absence of accountability.

## Mechanic
LadBible submitted an official application to the United Nations to recognise the Pacific Garbage Patch as a sovereign nation — The Trash Isles — complete with a flag (plastic bags), passport, currency (debris), stamps, and a national anthem. Al Gore became Citizen #1. Over 100,000 people signed for citizenship. The application was formally submitted to the UN.

## Why it worked
By giving an amorph problem the institutional form of a nation-state, the campaign created a story with a subject, a flag, and a legal hook that every news outlet could report on. Brand as activist (P04): a real institutional action at genuine cost. User as co-author (P09): each citizenship registration was a piece of content and a data point for the petition.

## Steal
Turn an invisible, amorph problem into a formal institutional object — a country, a corporation, a legal case. This creates a subject that press and public can interact with. The legal absurdity amplifies, not undermines, the seriousness of the underlying issue.

# 100 (Leica · 2014)

## Insight

Photography's power is not in the camera but in the moment it captures. The greatest photographs in history were possible because someone was there, with the right tool, at the exact right second.

## Mechanic

Film marking Leica's 100th anniversary by recreating iconic historical moments of the 20th century — showing what would have been lost if the photographer hadn't been there with a Leica. The camera is the hero because the moment is the hero.

## Why it worked

Craft as message meets long-form drama: a centenary film that made the camera's function inseparable from historical memory. By showing famous photographs being taken rather than the photographs themselves, Leica claimed credit for the moments, not just the images.

## Steal

For heritage brands, the product's history of use is often more powerful than its current capabilities. Make a film about what your product has been present for, not what it can do.

# Life's Adventure (Leroy Merlin · 2018)

## Insight
Home improvement isn't about the tools or materials — it's about what people build their lives into. The store is never the point; the life being constructed is.

## Mechanic
A long-form film following characters across significant life moments — the home as the stage for human drama. The home improvement retailer is present only through the backdrop of spaces being transformed and lives being lived in them.

## Why it worked
Long-form drama (P16) allows a DIY retailer to escape functional product territory entirely and occupy emotional territory — belonging, aspiration, growth — that makes the brand feel like a life partner rather than a hardware store. The product is invisible; the promise is everything.

## Steal
Retail brands can exit product communication entirely through long-form storytelling that positions them as enablers of life chapters rather than sellers of goods. The store becomes a supporting character, never the protagonist.

# Sweat it Out (Levi's · 2018)

## Insight

TODO: Year inferred; verify against campaign records.

Effort and physical work are where authentic character is built — denim is the fabric of people who do things with their hands and bodies, not just their screens.

## Mechanic

Film emphasizing physical effort, craft, and the satisfaction of working hard — featuring real people in real labor, positioning Levi's as the brand of people who sweat and build.

## Why it worked

Craft as message meets long-form drama: physical authenticity in fashion advertising is rare. Sweat, labor, and effort are category-disruptive when most denim advertising uses aspirational leisure contexts.

## Steal

Show the effort your product enables or endures, rather than the leisure it enables. Physical truth reads as authenticity in a category full of polished aspiration.
