# Rabbit (Skittles · 2010)

## Insight
Animals + Skittles absurdism = reliable territory for the brand's "Touch the Rainbow" platform. The internal logic simply applies: contact with Skittles causes impossible things to happen.

## Mechanic
A short absurdist film featuring a rabbit in Skittles' established surrealist framework. Exact details require source verification.

## Why it worked
Skittles' absurdist platform is broad enough to accommodate any starting premise. The brand's consistent internal logic means each new execution is instantly recognizable as a Skittles film, even without a logo.

## Steal
A strong creative platform should be able to accommodate almost any starting point. If your brand idea can only work with specific executions, the idea isn't strong enough.

# Telephone (Skittles · 2011)

## Insight
TODO: Verify exact year. Part of the Skittles absurdist "Touch the Rainbow" era. "Telephone" as a premise suggests a communication or chain-reaction format.

## Mechanic
An absurdist spot using telephone communication as the setup — likely a game of telephone gone surreal, or a phone conversation interrupted by impossible Skittles-related transformations. The deadpan commitment to an illogical premise is the Skittles creative signature.

## Why it worked
Phone calls provide a two-party structure that absurdist comedy can exploit — the gap between what one party says and what the other hears, or the impossibility of communicating the absurd via a normal channel. Skittles used mundane communication formats to make the absurdity feel grounded.

## Steal
Use the structure of everyday communication (phone call, letter, text) as the frame for impossible events. The banality of the medium contrasts with the absurdity of the content in a way that amplifies both.

# Touch (Skittles · 2012)

## Insight
The "Touch the Rainbow" platform gave Skittles a literal mechanic: touch = Skittles = transformation. Any execution simply applies a new object or situation to this formula.

## Mechanic
A short absurdist spot in the Skittles "Touch" series, where contact with Skittles causes an impossible transformation. The exact scenario requires source verification.

## Why it worked
The internal consistency of Skittles' absurdist world means audiences know the rules — and enjoy seeing them applied to new, increasingly strange scenarios. Each "Touch" spot builds brand equity through accumulated surprise.

## Steal
A strong, specific absurdist mechanic (touch transforms) is infinitely extensible. Each new execution is a new application of the same formula — the brand's consistency is the platform.

# Tube Sock (Skittles · 2009)

## Insight
Everyday mundane objects — a tube sock — become strange when Skittles logic is applied. The brand's absurdism operates by taking the most ordinary starting point and following it to an impossible conclusion.

## Mechanic
A short absurdist film featuring a tube sock in a Skittles scenario, following the brand's "Touch the Rainbow" logic.

## Why it worked
Mundane starting points (a tube sock, a man, a house) make the absurdist destination more surprising by contrast. The gap between ordinary premise and impossible conclusion is where Skittles' humor lives.

## Steal
The more mundane the setup, the more effective the absurdist punchline. Don't start strange — start familiar and go strange.

# Blueberries (Skoda · 2009)

## Insight
Skoda's tone is warm, slightly absurdist, and self-aware. The brand doesn't take itself seriously enough to be portentous — and that restraint is itself distinctive in automotive advertising.

## Mechanic
A film in Skoda's characteristically dry, affectionate register — likely involving an incongruous domestic detail (blueberries) applied to automotive context in a way that creates gentle comedy without resolution.

## Why it worked
Skoda's tonal consistency — across multiple executions — built a recognisable brand voice that distinguished it from the aspirational posturing of competitors. Any single execution benefited from the accumulated tonal equity.

## Steal
Tonal consistency across small, domestic, affectionate executions can build more brand equity than a single expensive epic. The accumulation is the strategy.
