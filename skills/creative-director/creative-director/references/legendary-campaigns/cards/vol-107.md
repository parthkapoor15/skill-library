# Facebook Not Live (unknown · 2017)

## Insight
TODO: Verify brand. The execution riffs on the Facebook Live format — the ubiquitous live-streaming feature — by presenting something deliberately un-live or anti-climactic, puncturing the platform's inherent self-importance.

## Mechanic
A film or series that parodies the Facebook Live format, subverting the expectation of urgency and importance that the "Live" label confers on trivial or absurd content.

## Why it worked
Absurd as carrier (P11): the gap between the "Live" gravitas and the mundane or ridiculous content is the joke. Cultural hijack (P05): the campaign borrows the visual and functional language of a dominant platform and inverts it. Platform fluency is the creative tool.

## Steal
Any format that carries inherent urgency or status (Live, Breaking News, Alert) can be subverted by filling it with anti-climactic content. The gap between signal and noise is the comedy.

# The Man Who Couldn't Slow Down (unknown · 2013)

## Insight
TODO: Verify brand. The film explores a character archetype familiar to the financial services audience — a high-achiever who cannot stop, cannot retire, cannot slow down — and uses this as an entry point into a conversation about life planning and financial wellbeing.

## Mechanic
A long-form film following a protagonist defined by relentless forward motion — professionally successful but emotionally stranded — whose arc invites reflection on what wealth is actually for.

## Why it worked
Long-form drama (P16): the character study requires extended runtime to earn the emotional weight of the insight. Truth telling (P13): the archetype is recognizable to the target audience — successful people who have not asked the bigger questions. Finance advertising that sells meaning rather than products.

## Steal
In financial services, the most effective insight is not about money management but about what money cannot manage. The character who has everything except an answer to "what now?" is a universal and underused protagonist for this category.

# The Piccards (unknown · 2018)

## Insight
TODO: Verify brand. A character-driven long-form film using a family narrative to explore how a technology or telecom product changes the texture of everyday life.

## Mechanic
Long-form character study following a family — possibly named the Piccards — navigating modern life with the product integrated into their story. The family drama format is the vehicle for product demonstration.

## Why it worked
Long-form drama (P16): the family unit is the most universal narrative container for technology communication. Family stories can compress years of change into minutes while maintaining emotional credibility. Character-led demonstration > feature-led demonstration.

## Steal
Technology adoption is always a family story — one member is the early adopter, one is resistant, children adapt fastest. The drama of that dynamic is more compelling than any spec sheet.
